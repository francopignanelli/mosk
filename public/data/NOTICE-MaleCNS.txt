MaleCNS v1.0 annotation sample

Source dataset: MaleCNS v1.0
Creators: FlyEM / HHMI Janelia Research Campus, University of Cambridge,
MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, and Google Research.
Publication: Berg et al. (2026), Sexual dimorphism in the complete connectome
of the Drosophila male central nervous system.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.015
Project and official data: https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

MOSK modifications: selected 96 annotation rows and listed fields from the
official body-annotations-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather file; converted
body IDs to decimal strings; added exact group counts, headings, and source
metadata. No connection weights, activity, or functional interpretations
were added. The source URL, SHA-256, selection rule and counts are embedded
in malecns-v1.0-atlas.json. Reproduce with scripts/prepare-malecns-atlas.py.

This anatomical reference is not a complete circuit, a trained behavioral
model, or a memory system, and does not control the MOSK fly.
The original dataset authors do not endorse MOSK.
